One race. Four audiences.

Sri Lanka

Runners, cyclists, swimmers, triathletes, clubs, gyms and first-time endurance athletes.

“Your home. Your challenge. 70.3.”

India

Our biggest acquisition market, with city-specific campaigns and packaged race travel.

“Mumbai → Colombo. Your next 70.3.”

Asia-Pacific

Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia and Australia, sold as a tropical 70.3 with a strong destination experience.

Europe & Middle East

The race paired with a 5–10 day Sri Lankan holiday.

“Race in Colombo. Stay in Sri Lanka.”

Paid targeting starts with the brief's priority markets: India, UAE, Singapore and Malaysia. Other markets are tested only where budget and results justify it.

27 — How we work

Our monthly workflow.

The same five steps, every month, from October to August.

Step 1Plan

Monthly content calendar and shot list, matched to the rollout phase. Approved by Serendib.

Step 2Shoot

Planned shoots of athletes, training, Colombo locations and partners, with our team on site.

Step 3Review

Edits shared for sign-off before anything goes live, within two business days.

Step 4Publish

Posting, partner collab posts, community replies and paid boosts where agreed.

Step 5Report

Monthly report on results, spend and best content, with weekly updates in the final push.

On the ground at key moments

Our team attends in person to capture content and cover live, subject to Serendib's requirements and approval. Race week has a designated on-call approver.
